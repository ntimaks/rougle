# Project rules

## Color
- **Never place lime (`#C8FF1F` / `#D4E635`) on white or paper (`#EEEEEE`, `#CACAC8`).** Contrast is too low. Same applies to amber `#E8B23A` and vermillion `#FF5D4A` against white.
- Filled accent surfaces take an **ink border** (`#0C0C0C`), never a white one. On the charcoal ground the white stamp shadow still sits outside the ink border, so the stamp offset reads without the accent ever touching white.
- Accent-on-ink and ink-on-accent are the only legal accent pairings.

## Rougle (the game)
- No light/dark toggle. The game is a single dark "terminal instrument" surface.
- Mobile-first. Hit targets never below 44px.
