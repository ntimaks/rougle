# v2.0 balance snapshot 003 — the last two valves, the board budget, and the calibration

Follows `v2-002.md`. Four items: the two valves that snapshot found, the board
budget the per-relic one implied, and the calibration underneath all of it.

## The calibration was six hundredths hot — R-049

Everything else here rests on one number, so it went first.

§11.4 tunes the handicap until `cleanBaseline` reads 3.9, probing **400 words**.
At that size the estimator carries about ±0.05, from two independent sources:

| source | spread at n=1500 |
|---|---|
| which words are drawn | 3.879 vs 3.796 between two samples |
| which vocabulary slice the seed produces | 3.838 / 3.781 / 3.855 on the *same* words |

So the search stopped at 3.893 while the converged value was **3.84** — and the
Gate 1 test guarding it probed the same 400 words and passed throughout.

| n | old knobs | new knobs |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | 3.888 | 3.973 |
| 1500 | 3.879 | 3.907 |
| 5000 | **3.841** | **3.868** |

Re-derived at n=2500: `vocabularyGap` 0.0938 → **0.1125**. `calibration.json` now
records `wordsPerProbe`, and the test reads that field instead of hardcoding 400,
so the check and the calibration cannot drift apart again.

## §6.7's forge — R-046

Sold bankroll at a flat 20g, *"any quantity affordable"*, with a forge every act.
After the R-042 and R-043 repricings it was the cheapest bankroll in the game and
the only uncapped one.

| | no relics |
|---|---|
| the two ladders alone | 34.3% |
| + forge at 20g uncapped | **63.5%** |
| + forge **on the shared ladder** | **34.3%** |

Ruled: the forge draws from the **same §4.1 ladder**. A run buys at most six
bankroll however it splits them between shop and forge, so the forge row is now
identical to the no-forge row. Pricing it separately would have re-opened the
arbitrage the moment either number moved again.

## §7's length scaling — R-047

Base rose by 1 a letter. Longer words take **fewer** guesses, so the base was
compounding an advantage rather than compensating a cost.

| Length | before | after (flat base 6) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | −1.54 | −1.54 |
| 6 · Long Word | **+0.07** | −0.94 |
| 7 · Longer Word | **+1.80** | −0.20 |

Long words in the run model: 75.5% → **64.8%**, against a 32.0% baseline. The
residue is not the payout's fault — §10's six- and seven-letter lists are ~800
and ~500 against ~1500, so there is less to disambiguate. That is a curation job.

Flattening the base also removed Clamp A's only bite on unassisted play: a
7-letter hole-in-one used to net +7 and be clamped to +5, so the luckiest guess
in the game was the one thing the clamp punished.

## The board is the budget — R-048

R-044 set an allowance per relic. §6.2 gives five slots, so that does not
constrain what §11.5 measures:

| Board | at R-044's 0.25/0.4/0.7/0.9 | at 0.20/0.30/0.40/0.50 |
|---|---|---|
| 5 common | 1.25 · dies | 1.00 · dies |
| 3 common + 2 uncommon | 1.55 · in band | 1.20 · dies |
| 2 uncommon + 3 rare | 2.90 · **immortal** | 1.80 · in band |
| 2 uncommon + 2 rare + 1 boss | 3.10 · **immortal** | 1.90 · in band |
| 1 uncommon + 2 rare + 2 boss | 3.60 · **immortal** | 2.10 · in band |

The tier table is now **derived** from "a board of five totals at most 1.2 × the
bleed" rather than chosen. The per-relic ceiling falls 0.9 → **0.50**.

### A payout bonus cannot be common

The "solve in three" trigger fires on 43.3% of words, so the smallest integer
bonus on it is **0.43** — the boss allowance — however small the bonus. The next
tighter trigger fires on 7.7%: +2 there is 0.15, +3 is 0.23. Nothing in between.

So a payout relic is either boss-tier or gated on a two-guess solve. `RL.11`
Flywheel is COMMON and now reads *"solve in two or fewer and the payout rises by
2"*. `CH.02` The Gambler keeps the three-guess trigger at +1 and is valued at the
boss allowance, because an innate is held for the whole run.

## Where §11.5 stands

| metric | v2-001 | v2-002 | now | target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| win rate, no relics | 91.5% | 34.3% | **32.0%** | <2% |
| poorest legal run | 85.8% | 21.5% | **21.8%** | <2% |

The forge and length fixes were worth about 2 points between them at the headline,
because neither was in the default row — the forge was off by default and long
words are a separate row. Their value is that the numbers are now honest: the
forge can no longer be arbitraged and Act III no longer pays a bonus.

**The remaining gap to 2% is not another valve.** With every valve priced, a
relic-less run still finishes 32% of the time on a start of 12, three boss clears
and six purchasable bankroll against a 20-word bleed of ~31. The arithmetic says
that should not survive — and it does, because 4.06 guesses a word is the *mean*
and the distribution's left tail (7.7% of words in two, 35.7% in three) carries
enough runs. Closing it means either a smaller start, fewer boss payouts, or
accepting that <2% is the wrong target for a 20-word run with this much variance.
That is a design call, not a measurement.

## Still open

- **The engine has not migrated.** Everything here is the model. Sixteen of the
  twenty-two relics change guesses-per-word or gold and cannot be valued at all
  until it does, and §11.5's 20–30% tuned-build target needs all five archetypes.
- **§10's 6- and 7-letter lists** are half and a third the size of the 5-letter
  list, which is the whole of the remaining length advantage.
- **§11.5's <2% no-relic target** against a 20-word run — see above.
