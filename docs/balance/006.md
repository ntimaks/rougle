# Balance snapshot 006 — the act pools, re-derived

1000 runs, `npm run sim -- --runs 1000 --snapshot 006`. Supersedes `005.md`.

Snapshot 005 said the §2.2 budgets were the first thing to re-derive, because
every one of them was set against a board that lied to the player until R-036.
This is that work.

## Pools: 22/19/17 → **19/12/14**

Swept sequentially — deaths in an act depend on that act's pool, so Act I first,
then Act II given Act I, then Act III. Full curves in the PR; the shape:

| Act I pool | 22 | 20 | **19** | 18 | 17 | 16 | 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| act I deaths | 1.8% | 6.0% | **8.1%** | 17.0% | 22.0% | 31.2% | 41.4% |

| Act II pool | 19 | 17 | 16 | 15 | 14 | 13 | **12** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| act II deaths | 2.0% | 4.2% | 7.6% | 12.0% | 16.4% | 22.4% | **28.6%** |

**Act II was the whole problem.** At pool 19 it killed 2% of runs while being
nominally the tightest act in the game. Its measured need is 20.2 guesses
(4 × 3.78 + the Twins at 5.05) and it had 19 — a deficit of 1.2 that refunds and
relics erased completely. It is now the act a run has to survive.

## Act III's pool is not a difficulty lever

| Act III pool | 17 | 15 | 14 | 13 | 12 | 11 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| win rate | 51.2% | 52.4% | 51.2% | 52.0% | 53.4% | 52.2% | 50.0% |
| act III deaths | 14.4% | 13.4% | 12.8% | 14.4% | 12.8% | 13.8% | 15.4% |

Flat across a seven-point range. Act III's four solve nodes cost 13.3 guesses,
and everything that kills there is the Gauntlet, which runs on its own separate
pool of 14. Emergency purchases rise only 0.89 → 1.00 as the pool falls 17 → 10,
so the ladder absorbs a little of the cut, but mostly the pool was never binding.

Set to **14** — its measured need — so the number in §2.2 means something
instead of carrying three guesses of dead headroom. This is truth in labelling,
not a difficulty change, and the table above is the evidence.

## Gate 3 — restored

600 runs, `--no-relics`:

| | win rate | died I | died II | died III |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 005 (22/19/17) | 54.6% | 0.8% | 4.2% | 40.4% |
| **now (19/12/14)** | **23.5%** | 4.5% | **57.3%** | 14.7% |

A build-less run now dies in Act II — modally and by a wide margin (344 of 459
deaths). The gate as §2.2 states it ("a player with no relics should die in Act
II") **passes**. §10.3's stricter numeric — a no-relic win rate under 5% — does
not: 23.5%. Relics are worth 27.7 points (51.2% vs 23.5%), against ten in 004.

## The word-luck metric was measuring the wrong thing

005 reported 74.5% word-luck deaths, and §10.3 says of that number: *"fix the
list, not the budget."* Before acting on it I broke down what those deaths
actually were, over 607 of them:

| candidates left at death | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| answer already eliminated | 22.7% | decision quality |
| exactly 1 | 12.0% | pure budget — knew the word, no guess left |
| **2–5** | **27.3%** | **the §8.1 coin-flip** |
| 6+ (over half at the 20 cap) | 37.9% | budget — field still wide open |

The metric counted everything from 2 upward, merging "forced to coin-flip
between four near-twins" with "the pool ran out while twenty words were still
live". Those move in *opposite* directions when pools are tuned: tightening a
pool kills more runs early with the field wide, so the number that is supposed
to indict the WORD LIST rises when the BUDGET changes.

Bounded to 2–5 now, with the wide-field deaths printed on their own line
(§13 I-29). Word luck is **28.9%**, not 74.5%. Still five times the target, but
it is now a number that means what §10.3 says it means — and a re-curation pass
launched on the 74.5% figure would have been chasing a budget problem through
the word list.

## What pools cannot do

The win rate is 51.2% against a 25–35% target, and **pools cannot close that**.
The floor, holding Act I's death rate under §10.3's 15% ceiling:

| pools | win | act I deaths | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19/13/14 | 53.0% | 10.0% | |
| **19/12/14** | **50.3%** | **9.0%** | chosen |
| 18/13/14 | 50.3% | 13.5% | |
| 18/12/14 | 48.0% | 12.5% | on the ceiling |
| 17/12/14 | 45.2% | 19.5% | breaks §10.3 |
| 18/11/14 | 42.5% | 15.2% | breaks §10.3 |

19/12/14 over 18/12/14 for 2.3 points: 12.5% ± 1.4 at 600 runs sits on a hard
limit, and Act I is the act §2.2 asks to *teach*. Act II carries the difficulty
instead, which is where the design wants it.

Closing the remaining 16 points needs a lever that is not the pool. In order of
what the data supports:

1. **Gold.** Mean 297 unspent at death, and 61.5% of deaths still hold 25g or
   more. Gold converts into both pool (§2.3) and information (§2.5), so surplus
   gold is a difficulty leak that no pool number can plug. `goldPerLeftoverGuess`
   and the reward figures have never been swept.
2. **The word list (§8.1).** 28.9% word luck against <5% is the largest single
   miss on the board, and §10.3 is explicit that this one is fixed by curation.
3. **Modifier frequency and stacking.** Never swept; §3.1's node weights and
   §5's stacking rules are both untouched since they were written.

Do **not** reach for the pools again first. They are now measured, and the two
findings above (Act III is inert, Act I is capped) mean there is very little
left in them.
