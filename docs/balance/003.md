## Gate 3 — "does a no-relic bot die in Act II?"

**Met, with a caveat worth reading.** 500 runs with `--no-relics`:

```
win rate                33.6%
deaths by act    I 24    II 156    III 152
EMERGENCY_UNAFFORDABLE  166
GAUNTLET                135
POOL_EXHAUSTED           31
```

It dies in Act II in 31% of runs and two thirds of runs end before the Gauntlet,
so the budgets are not trivially generous. The caveat: a bot holding **nothing**
still wins a third of the time, and its win rate (33.6%) is inside the §10.3
target that the relic-holding bot (43.7%) misses. Relics are worth about ten
points of win rate. That is a smaller contribution than a game whose entire power
axis is relics should want, and it is the number to watch when the remaining
three relics land.

## What these numbers are not

The bot takes relics until it holds four, then banks gold. It always walks the
first available node, and always picks the non-destructive event option. Route
choice, event gambling and activation timing are all therefore unmeasured floors,
not play. The win rate is one point on a curve, not the game's difficulty.
