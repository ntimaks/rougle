# v2.0 balance snapshot 002 — the four review decisions, measured

`npx tsx sim/v2/report.ts 400`. Follows `v2-001.md`, which measured v2.0's economy
and found that a relic-less run wins 91.5% against a §11.5 target of 2%.

Four decisions came back from design review. Three were straightforward to apply.
The fourth turned into a sweep, and the sweep found two more.

## 1 · The refill valve — R-042

Applied: **1 per shop, 40/60/80/100/120/140g across the run, hard cap 6.**

| | no relics |
|---|---|
| flat 25g, 3 a shop (was) | **91.0%** |
| the ladder | **34.3%** |
| no refills at all | 23.0% |

The valve is now worth about 11 points instead of 68. 540g for 6 bankroll is 90g
each against relic efficiency of ~27g, which is the intended direction: gold buys
builds, and buys survival at a penalty.

## 2 · The emergency ladder — R-043

Applied: **80/160/320, +3 each, three rungs.** The grant was right and the price
had not followed it: at 25g for +3 the first purchase was 8.3g a bankroll, the
cheapest in the game, which made running yourself to zero the optimal line.

Mean emergency purchases move from 0.03 to 0.92 — the ladder went from an
irrelevance a well-funded run never touched to something most runs use once.

## 3 · Silent Start — R-045

Already ruled, twice, as **R-028** and **R-029**. The review's premise was that
the log ends at R-021; it does in the v2.0 document, which was drafted from v1.1,
but the repository shipped v1.2 and v1.3. The diagnosis offered — that a false
`GREY` feeds `RL.02` The Sieve and locks a solution letter — is R-029's own
reasoning, and R-029 closed it by making suppressed tiles report `UNKNOWN`.

The one substantive change proposed, rendering the suppressed yellow `HIDDEN`
rather than `UNKNOWN`, is **rejected on a collision**: `HIDDEN` already means
*this whole row is unread* (Fog, the Cipher), and `consistent()` bails on the
entire row the moment it sees one. A per-tile `HIDDEN` would discard the greens
and greys on the same row, and with them the deduction that a blank tile on a
Silent Start row **is** a withheld yellow — an inference worth 21 measured points
of win rate (§13 I-27). If `UNKNOWN` carrying both "decayed green" and "withheld
yellow" is too overloaded, the answer is a new `TileState`, not the one Fog owns.

No code changed. This is the one of the four that was already right.

## 4 · The relic value budget — R-044

Added to §6.4: an allowance by rarity (0.25 / 0.4 / 0.7 / 0.9 bankroll per word)
and a hard ceiling of half the bleed. Stated against §2.3's own 3.9 baseline —
bleed 1.8, ceiling 0.9 — rather than a measured sample, because a budget that
moves when someone re-runs the harness is not a budget, and because half of 1.8
is exactly the boss allowance. Measured against 1500 real solves the bleed is
1.55 and the ceiling would be 0.78, below the boss tier, so no boss relic could
reach its own allowance.

**Nine of the ten measurable relics were over the ceiling.**

| relic | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| `RL.19` The Moth | **2.50** | 0.50 |
| `RL.19` The Moth MK.II | **2.60** | 0.60 |
| `RL.11` Flywheel MK.II | **1.61** | 0.80 |
| `RL.15` Bloodhound MK.II | **1.50** | 0.60 |
| `CH.02` The Gambler | **1.30** | 0.87 |
| `RL.13` Opening Gambit | **0.98** | 0.43 |
| `RL.15` Bloodhound | **0.90** | 0.30 |
| `RL.11` Flywheel | **0.87** | 0.43 |
| `RL.12` Hot Streak | 0.77 | 0.72 |
| `RL.31` Rosetta Slab | −1.00 | −1.00 |

`RL.19` The Moth was the outlier by a distance: **2.50 bankroll a word, more than
the entire bleed, on an UNCOMMON**, because it paid +2 at *every* word start
rather than on the gorge. Hot Streak's uncapped cumulative streak is now capped
at +3; Opening Gambit's "four or more unique letters" was never a condition — a
strong opener meets it essentially always — so it is now gated on the solve as
well.

Everything measurable is under the ceiling. Several sit over their rarity tier,
which is the next finding.

### The COMMON tier is below the granularity of a payout bonus

The smallest integer bonus is +1. The natural trigger, solving in three or fewer,
fires on **43.3%** of words — 0.43 a word, against a COMMON allowance of 0.25.
Tightening the trigger to two guesses drops it to 7.7% of words and **0.08**.
There is nothing in between.

So a COMMON payout relic can be worth 0.43 or 0.08 and nothing near 0.25. Either
the COMMON allowance rises to about 0.45, or COMMON relics use a lever other than
the payout. Unresolved.

Sixteen of the twenty-two relics change **guesses per word** or **gold** instead
and cannot be valued by arithmetic at all — they need the engine. The budget
binds them; it just cannot check them yet.

## Two more the sweep found

### §6.7's forge is now the cheapest and only uncapped bankroll in the game

Operation B converts gold to bankroll at **20g, "any quantity affordable"**, with
at least one forge an act. After R-042 and R-043 that undercuts every rung of
both ladders, and unlike them it has no cap.

| | no relics |
|---|---|
| the ladders alone | 34.3% |
| **+ §6.7 B at 20g, uncapped** | **63.5%** |

It undoes most of the repricing on its own. It needs the same treatment the
other two valves just got: a price above relic efficiency, and a cap.

### §7's two difficulty modifiers are rewards

Longer words are *easier* for a solver — more letters, more constraints per
guess, faster narrowing — while §2.3's base rises by 1 per length. 800 real
solves per length:

| Length | Base | Guesses/word | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 6 | 3.77 | **−1.54** |
| 6 · Long Word, act II+ | 7 | 3.47 | **+0.07** |
| 7 · Longer Word, act III | 8 | 3.10 | **+1.80** |

A 7-letter word is worth **+1.80 bankroll** where a 5-letter word costs 1.54, so
Act III's headline difficulty modifier is the most profitable thing in the game.
It shows up in the run model too: turning long words on takes a relic-less run
from 34.3% to 75.5%.

Either the base stops rising with length — flat 6 makes a 7-letter word net −0.2,
still the hardest of the three — or these two stop being called difficulty.

## Where §11.5 stands

| metric | v2-001 | now | target |
|---|---|---|---|
| win rate, no relics | 91.5% | **34.3%** | <2% |
| win rate, no relics, poorest legal run | 85.8% | **21.5%** | <2% |

Still an order of magnitude off, and the two findings above are why: the forge is
an open valve worth ~29 points, and Act III's words pay a bonus. Neither was
visible until the other two ladders were priced correctly — the refill valve was
large enough to hide both.

## Still open

- **§6.7 B and §7's length scaling**, above. Both are arithmetic that was carried
  across the redesign without being redone, the same shape as R-042.
- **The COMMON granularity problem.**
- **The engine has still not migrated.** Everything here is the model.
- **§11.4's calibration reads 3.89 at n=400 and 3.77 at n=1500** on the same
  solver. The handicap is tuned to hit 3.9, so if the estimator is that noisy the
  handicap is mis-set. Worth a look before the next tuning pass leans on it.
