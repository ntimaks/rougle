# Balance snapshot v2-002 — rarity gates supply, price does not

1000 runs, `npm run sim -- --runs 1000 --gate3 --snapshot v2-002`. The live
engine, not the v2.0 spec model — `v2-001.md` measures a different thing and the
two do not compare. Reads against snapshot `007.md`.

Ruled as MECHANICS.md R-042 and R-043; the reasoning is `ADR-0013`.

## Rarity was decoration

`rarity` coloured a card spine and looked up a price. No draw consulted it.
`rollOffer` and `rollShopStock` weighted by archetype and nothing else, so a
tier's share of the shelf was simply how many relics happened to be in it.

400 seeds per act, relic slots only:

| | Act I | Act II | Act III | | Act I | Act II | Act III |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | **before** | | | | **after** | | |
| COMMON | 27.9% | 29.4% | 28.5% | | 59.9% | 41.4% | 25.7% |
| UNCOMMON | 45.7% | 42.6% | 43.7% | | 30.3% | 43.1% | 46.9% |
| RARE | **23.4%** | **23.0%** | **24.4%** | | **9.8%** | **15.5%** | **27.4%** |
| BOSS | 3.0% | 5.0% | 3.4% | | 0% | 0% | 0% |

Three readings of the before column:

1. **7 of 27 offerable relics are RARE, and RARE filled 23% of slots.** That is
   what "no weighting" predicts to the decimal. A rare turned up in roughly two
   of every three three-card offers.
2. **The three columns are the same column.** A run in Act III drew from the
   shelf it drew from in Act I.
3. **Boss relics leaked into the ordinary pool**, and `PRICE_BASE` had no `BOSS`
   row, so they fell through to `COMMON`. `RL.29` The Mask was a 55g shop staple
   with the highest pick rate in the game.

The after column is within 0.6 points of `cfg.rarityWeights` in every cell. The
drift is §6.6's archetype bias, which is intended and multiplies on top.

## What a shelf costs

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| COMMON | 55g (45–65) | **35g** (30–40) |
| UNCOMMON | 90g (70–110) | **60g** (50–70) |
| RARE | 150g (120–180) | **95g** (80–110) |
| BOSS | *55g — fell through to COMMON* | **140g** (120–160) |
| CONSUMABLE | 45g (35–55) | **30g** (25–35) |
| whole shelf, Act I | 5 slots, 426g | **4 slots, 176g** |
| whole shelf, Act III | 5 slots, 434g | **4 slots, 220g** |

Against roughly 300g of income an act. Variance is ±15% rather than ±20%, which
makes the bands disjoint — at ±20% a lucky COMMON outpriced an unlucky UNCOMMON.

## The change is worth 6.5 points, and both are spent

Same 1000 seeds throughout.

| | win | act I deaths | relics/run |
|---|---|---|---|
| 007's config, re-measured on these seeds | 33.9% | 10.2% | — |
| + rarity weights, cheaper prices, reserved consumable slot | **40.4%** | 9.8% | 9.1 |

(007 published 33.4% and 10.7% on its own seeds. 33.9% is the same config on
this snapshot's, and is the number every row below compares against.)

Out of §10.3's 25–35% band. 600-run sweep of the ways back:

| lever | win | act I deaths | relics/run |
|---|---|---|---|
| as changed | 40.2% | 9.7% | 9.1 |
| `shopRelicSlots` 4 → 3 | 37.0% | 9.0% | 8.6 |
| prices +15% | 36.2% | 9.3% | 8.5 |
| `gauntlet.pool` 10 → 9 | 36.7% | 9.7% | 9.0 |
| reward gold 20/40/60 → 15/30/50 | 34.2% | **14.7%** | 8.3 |
| slots 3 + reward gold cut | 32.8% | **14.8%** | 7.9 |
| act II pool 12 → 11, slots 3 | 34.7% | 9.0% | 8.2 |
| **slots 3 + `gauntlet.pool` 9** | **33.2%** | **9.0%** | **8.5** |
| slots 3 + `gauntlet.pool` 8 | 30.2% | 9.0% | 8.5 |

**Gold income is not the lever.** Cutting reward gold reaches the band and takes
Act I's death rate to 14.7% against a 15% target doing it. Early gold buys the
first emergency guess; removing it tunes the win rate by killing players in Act
I, which §10.3 forbids directly.

**Act II's pool is not the lever either.** 11 puts 41.5% of all runs' deaths in
Act II against Act III's 14.8% — a middle act that ends two runs in five and a
final act that ends one in seven.

**`shopRelicSlots` 3 is free**, because §4.1 already said 3 relics and 1
consumable. −3.2 points and Act I does not move.

**`gauntlet.pool` 9 is 007's held lever, and this is what it was held for.** 007
measured 9, saw it land at 28.2%, and kept 10 because the band's floor buys
nothing. The +6.5 points here is exactly the headroom that makes 9 sit mid-band
instead. Act I and Act II are unmoved at any setting, as 007 found.

## §10.3 targets

| metric | 007 as published | now | target | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| win rate | 33.4% | **33.2%** | 25–35% | OK |
| act I death rate | 10.7% | **9.4%** | <15% | OK |
| word-luck deaths | 23.7% | **20.8%** | <5% of deaths | OFF |
| median emergency purchases | 1 | **1** | >0 | OK |
| win rate, no relics taken | 7.9%\* | **0.0%**\* | <5% | OK |
| gate 3 — no-relic runs die in | act II | **act II** | act II | OK |

Deaths by act: **I 94 · II 349 · III 225.** By cause: `EMERGENCY_UNAFFORDABLE`
411, `GAUNTLET` 216, `POOL_EXHAUSTED` 41. Act II is still the wall (§2.2's
intent) and Act III has not overtaken it, which was 007's stated worry about
`gauntlet.pool` 9.

\* **The two no-relic numbers do not compare, and neither does any earlier one.**
`--no-relics` skipped the reward screen and nothing else, so the no-relic bot
walked into every shop and bought relics off the shelf. At 55g and usually broke
that was worth little and stayed invisible; at 35g the same flag reported 16.2%.
Fixed in `sim/runner.ts`: `noRelics` now declines relics on a shelf too and buys
consumables and refills as before. 0.0% is the first honest reading of this
metric, and it says a relic-free run does not clear the Gauntlet. Snapshots
001–007's figures for it were measuring "no *free* relics".

## What this did not touch

**Word-luck deaths, 20.8% against <5%.** Still the only §10.3 metric off, still
§10.3's own instruction to fix the list rather than the budget. The one number
here that is not this change's to move.

**`RL.29` The Mask, 38.3% → 90.6% of runs.** It is the only implemented boss
relic (`RL.22`, `RL.30`, `RL.31` are in `PENDING_IMPLEMENTATION`), so every boss
offer leads with it, and the bot takes the first card on the table. Partly a bot
artefact and partly real: a guaranteed relic is not an exciting one. The fix is
content, and the number is worth re-reading when the other three land — at which
point the top-up from the ordinary pool disappears on its own and the boss offer
becomes the three-way choice §3.3 describes.

**The bot's reveal policy**, §13 I-22, still the largest single uncertainty in
the balance picture and still not resolvable by sweeping.

## Still open

1. **Gate 2 is a human question and this snapshot does not answer it.** The whole
   claim of this change is that a shelf you can act on is more interesting than a
   shelf you cannot, and that finding a RARE at 10% feels like a find. A bot that
   buys the cheapest affordable thing and takes the first card offered cannot
   report on either. It needs people playing.
2. **The act curve is a first guess.** 60/30/10 → 42/42/16 → 26/46/28 was written
   to a shape, not measured to one. `cfg.rarityWeights` is swept the same way any
   other tunable is; nobody has swept it.
3. **Per-relic pricing.** `CN.03` The Decanter pours 3 guesses for 30g, where the
   emergency ladder charges 25g for one. A flat price per rarity cannot express
   that, and this change did not make it worse — it was 45g against the same
   ladder before — but it is the clearest case that the flat table has a limit.
