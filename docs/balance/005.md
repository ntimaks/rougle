# Balance snapshot 005 — R-036, and the reveal ladder that was a trap

1000 runs, `npm run sim -- --runs 1000 --snapshot 005`. Supersedes `004.md`.

R-036 stopped a bought reveal from stamping a green over every row. The
correctness case is in MECHANICS §11; this records what it did to the numbers,
which is more than anyone expected.

## The reveal ladder was a 12.8-point penalty for using it

Wiring `--no-reveals` (§13 I-28 — the flag was parsed nowhere, so every previous
"with vs without" comparison was the same sweep twice) makes the A/B possible
for the first time. 600 runs each:

| | reveals off | reveals on | ladder is worth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **before R-036** | 55.8% | **43.0%** | **−12.8 points** |
| **after R-036** | 70.8% | **76.7%** | **+5.9 points** |

A row is read against the word that was typed. A green at index 0 of a row
guessing `TRAIN` asserts the answer starts with T — contradicting the reveal
that produced it, which said N. So every purchase eliminated the true answer
from anything reading the board, the solver included. §2.5 is the ladder R-020
built the entire gold economy around, and paying for it made you worse.

## §10.3 targets

| metric | 004 | now | target | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| win rate | 45.1% | **76.5%** | 25–35% | OFF, badly |
| act I death rate | 6.6% | **1.9%** | <15% | OK |
| word-luck deaths | 29.3% | **74.5%** | <5% of deaths | OFF |
| mean emergency purchases | 0.84 | **0.14** | >0 | OFF |

Every number moved the same way for the same reason: the board stopped lying
when you paid it. 004 and everything before it measured a game in which the
information economy was inverted.

## What that leaves — the game is now much too easy

Deaths by act: **I 19 · II 15 · III 201**. Of 235 deaths, 189 are the Gauntlet.
Acts I and II are no longer a threat at all, and the run is a corridor to one
boss.

**Gate 3 no longer holds.** 500 runs with `--no-relics`: a bot with no build at
all wins **54.6%** and dies **I 4 · II 21 · III 202**. The gate asks "does a
no-relic bot die in Act II?" and the answer is now no — it strolls to Act III.
Relics are worth **21.9 points** (76.5% vs 54.6%), which is a healthier spread
than 004's ten, but only because the floor rose.

`mean emergency purchases 0.14` and `deaths holding 25g or more 91.1%` say the
same thing from the economy side: nothing is contested any more. Gold spent and
gold unspent are both 507 — the bot ends every run with as much as it spent.

## Where the difficulty went

Guesses per word fell from 3.71 to **3.58**, and the calibration word from 3.82
to 3.58 — through the "strong solver 3.5" line and past the 3.9 human baseline.
The Cipher fell 6.15 → 5.22 guesses and the Twins 5.37 → 5.05.

This is not a solver that got smarter. It is a board that stopped
self-contradicting, and the previous numbers were paying a tax that R-036
removed. The §2.2 act budgets (22/19/17) were set against measurements that
included that tax, so **they are now the first thing to re-derive**, before any
other tuning: three pools sized for a game where information was poisoned.

## Recommended order

1. **Re-derive the §2.2 act budgets.** Everything else is downstream and every
   pool figure predates R-036.
2. **Then re-run Gate 3.** It is the gate that just broke, and a smaller pool is
   the most likely thing to restore it without touching a rule.
3. **Only then** revisit §13 I-25 (word-luck deaths, now 74.5%). That number is
   partly an artefact of almost every death being one boss: with 189 of 235
   deaths in the Gauntlet, "the answer was still open" describes the Gauntlet's
   own pool of 14, not the game.
