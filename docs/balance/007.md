# Balance snapshot 007 — the economy, the Gauntlet, and a bot that replays

1000 runs, `npm run sim -- --runs 1000 --snapshot 007`. Supersedes `006.md`.

Snapshot 006 closed by naming two things: the gold economy, and `gauntlet.pool`,
"still provisional and never swept". This is both. Neither turned out to be the
story.

## The measurement was broken first

An A/B on the economy came back with two arms four points of win rate apart.
The two arms were the same config by value. The solver's row-narrowing cache
keyed the bot's vocabulary by its **word count** — `vocabularyGap` gives every
run a different ~958-word slice of the same list, and equal-sized slices are
common — so one run narrowed against another run's vocabulary.

It stayed invisible for as long as the cache only grew, because an ambiguous key
is at least consistently wrong. It became visible when the 20,000-entry limit
cleared the cache: a lookup that used to hit now missed, and missing computed a
different answer. Two passes of an identical config over 400 seeds then
disagreed on **346 of them**.

The threshold is about 150 runs. So:

| | contaminated? |
|---|---|
| `sim/calibration.json` (`cleanBaseline`, ~40 entries) | no — byte-identical after the fix |
| the Gate 1 test (25 runs) | no — passed throughout, which is why nobody knew |
| every balance sweep of 200+ runs, snapshots 004–006 | **yes** |

Re-measured on the fixed bot, snapshot 006's conclusions survive intact: the
pool grid keeps the same ordering and the same choice (19/12/14), moving by
±2 points, and Gate 3 still passes. What does not survive is 006's *number* —
51.2% was 47.8% on the same seeds with the bot fixed.

Technical brief §13 I-30. The Gate 1 test now asserts the property rather than
the symptom: clearing the solver's caches between runs must not change any run.
That fails in 379ms at 25 runs; "repeat the sweep and compare" needed 200.

## Where gold actually comes from

Zeroing one source at a time, 600 runs each:

| source | gold/run | share |
|---|---|---|
| word nodes paying 40g instead of a relic (R-025) | 343 | **49.2%** |
| elite and boss rewards | 223 | 31.9% |
| leftover guesses at act end (§2.1) | 87 | 12.5% |
| events and relics | 44 | 6.4% |

006 nominated `goldPerLeftoverGuess` as the thing to sweep. It is 12.5% of the
economy and zeroing it entirely costs 1.8 points of win rate. The faucet is
R-025: **the economy runs on refusing relics**, and nothing else comes close.

## The economy cannot reach the band either

Each lever alone, from 52.0%, 600 runs each:

| lever | pushed to | win | act I deaths |
|---|---|---|---|
| word-node gold 40 → 15 | 15g | 43.7% | 12.8% |
| §2.3 emergency ladder ×4 | 100/200/400 | 43.3% | 9.3% |
| §2.5 reveal ladder ×4 | 80/200/480 | 45.2% | 12.8% |
| elite/boss gold 40/60 → 15/25 | | 48.0% | 8.0% |

Crossed 4×4, faucet against sink, the whole grid floors at **38%** — the
bottom-right quadrant is flat. Gold is rate-capped, not price-capped: three
emergency rungs an act, three reveals a word, and none at all in the Gauntlet.
Past a point more gold does nothing, and so less gold does nothing either.

The reveal ladder was rejected as a lever on top of that. §13 I-22 records that
the bot buys reveals only in desperation, so raising their price hurts the bot
less than it hurts a person — the one lever here that would land harder in real
play than in the sweep.

**So: pools floor at ~48%, pools plus the entire gold economy floor at ~38%,
and the target is 25–35%.**

## The Gauntlet pool was never a lever because it was never wired

A sweep of `gauntlet.pool` from 14 down to 8 returned six **byte-identical**
rows. That reads exactly like "not a difficulty lever" — the same conclusion 006
reached honestly about Act III's pool, and it would have been filed as one.

`BOSSES` is frozen at import against the default `CONFIG`, so `gauntlet.pool`,
`gauntlet.words` and `cipherDeferralDepth` reached the engine as constants and
the harness's override did nothing. `applyRefund` and `projectBoard` read the
module `CONFIG` too. Technical brief §13 I-31.

Wired up, it is the strongest lever in the game and the only clean one — the
full table is in MECHANICS.md §2.2. Three to five points of win rate per guess,
and Act I and Act II do not move by a tenth of a point at any setting, because
it is the last thing in the run.

## What shipped

| | 006 | 007 |
|---|---|---|
| `rewards.wordGoldInstead` | 40 | **20** |
| `gauntlet.pool` | 14 | **10** |
| `emergencyCosts` | 25/50/100 | unchanged |
| act pools | 19/12/14 | unchanged |

20g is one §2.5 reveal exactly: declining a relic buys one look at one letter.
At 40 it bought that *and* most of an emergency guess, so the gold was never
really an alternative to the relic — it was strictly better, and the bot banked
it every time. 10 is the Gauntlet against a measured need of 16.3.

Both were needed. `gauntlet.pool` alone reaches the band at 9 (32.9%), but Act
III then ends 27.7% of all runs against Act II's 31.7% — a final boss as deadly
as the act that is supposed to be the wall. Cutting the faucet as well buys the
same win rate with the deaths spread 10.7 / 36.4 / 19.5.

## §10.3 targets

| metric | 006 | now | target | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| win rate | 47.8%\* | **33.4%** | 25–35% | OK |
| act I death rate | 9.4%\* | **10.7%** | <15% | OK |
| word-luck deaths | 28.9% | **23.7%** | <5% of deaths | OFF |
| median emergency purchases | 1 | **1** | >0 | OK |
| win rate, no relics taken | — | **7.9%** | <5% | OFF by 2.9 |

\* 006's config re-measured on the fixed bot, same seeds. 006 reported 51.2%
and 7.5% off a contaminated one.

The no-relic target is in §10.3's table and the report had never printed it —
Gate 3 checked that a relic-less run *dies in Act II*, which it does emphatically
(64.9% of all runs), but not that it wins under 5%. At 7.9% it misses by 2.9
points. `gauntlet.pool` 9 would close it and take the main win rate to 28.2%,
which is still in band; that is a deliberate hold, not an oversight. The bot's
relic policy is to take the first of three offered and then bank gold, so a
no-relic run is the only run whose build the bot does not misrepresent — which
makes 7.9% the most trustworthy number in this snapshot and the one worth
respecting before tuning it away.

`npm run sim -- --gate3` now runs the no-relic sweep alongside the main one and
prints both §10.3 win-rate targets and the Gate 3 verdict. It had been re-run by
hand every snapshot, which is why half of it was never checked.

Deaths by act: **I 107 · II 364 · III 195**. By cause: `EMERGENCY_UNAFFORDABLE`
446, `GAUNTLET` 184, `POOL_EXHAUSTED` 36. Act II is still the wall, which is
§2.2's intent, and the Gauntlet is now a real final boss: two runs in three that
reach it clear it.

**Gate 3 passes.** A no-relic bot dies in Act II in 64.9% of all runs (I 10.5%,
III 16.7%). Relics are worth 25.5 points of win rate.

Economy: mean gold unspent at the end of a run 279 → **191**, and deaths holding the price
of an out 50.7% → **41.9%**. That metric now reads the ladder's first rung from
the config and excludes Gauntlet deaths — the Gauntlet has no ladder, so gold in
hand there was never an out that went unbought, and counting it made the number
get *worse* every time the Gauntlet got harder. Second metric in two snapshots to
be measuring something other than its name; §13 I-29 was the first.

## What is left

1. **The word list (§8.1).** 23.7% word luck against <5% is now the only §10.3
   metric still off, and §10.3 is explicit that this one is fixed by curation,
   not by tuning. It is the whole remaining gap.
2. **Modifier frequency and stacking.** Still never swept. §3.1's node weights
   and §5's stacking rules are untouched since they were written.
3. **`cipherDeferralDepth` and `gauntlet.words`** became reachable with I-31 and
   have still never been swept. Neither is a suspected problem; they are simply
   now measurable for the first time.

## Still open

- **§13 I-21** — one node ending a third of runs. Act II accounts for 364 of 666
  deaths and the Gauntlet for 184. Both are now the design's intent rather than
  an accident, but the concentration is worth re-reading with these numbers.
- **§13 I-22** — the bot's reveal policy is desperation-only, and the report's
  own caveat puts the win rate anywhere between 58.5% and 32.8% depending on it.
  Every number here is one point on that curve. This is the largest single
  uncertainty in the whole balance picture and it is not going to be resolved by
  another sweep — it needs a person playing.
